A pool plan on paper — the page chapter 4 asks for
====================================================================
Fill one of these in BEFORE running zpool create. The point
is not the form; it is that the decisions are made slowly,
in writing, rather than quickly at a prompt.

POOL NAME:        tank

THE DATA:         family photos, documents, media library,
                  backups of the household machines
THE PAIN:         losing the photos is the unacceptable
                  outcome; media can be re-ripped, slowly

DISKS (from /dev/disk/by-id/, serials copied off the labels):
  bay 1:  ata-...<model>-<serial-1>      4 TB   bought 2024
  bay 2:  ata-...<model>-<serial-2>      4 TB   bought 2024
  bay 3:  empty (the growth slot)
  bay 4:  empty

SHAPE:            one mirror vdev (bay 1 + bay 2)
                  survives: either disk dying
                  usable:   about half the raw 8 TB
WHY NOT RAIDZ:    with two disks there is nothing to
                  RAIDZ with; the mirror is also the shape
                  that grows by swapping disks two at a time

AT CREATION:      ashift=12, compression=lz4, atime=off,
                  xattr=sa, mountpoint=/tank

DATASETS:
  tank/photos     the irreplaceable stuff
  tank/documents  small, precious
  tank/media      recordsize=1M, short snapshot life
  tank/backups    quota per machine under it
  tank/scratch    sync=disabled, no tears shed

SNAPSHOTS:        automated (chapter 10): hourly x 24,
                  daily x 7, weekly x 4
SCRUBS:           monthly, first Sunday, and read the result
REPLICATION:      nightly send/receive to the second
                  machine's backup pool (chapter 10)

WHEN A DISK DIES: the map is this page. Bay and serial are
                  written above; chapter 8 is the procedure.
